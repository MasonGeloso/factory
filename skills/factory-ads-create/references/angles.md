# Ad angles — pick deliberately, and check against what already exists

Two ads that make the same argument in different words count as one ad. Vary the **lead**, not the
wording. A four-week test made of four benefit ads tests nothing.

## The angles

| Angle | Leads with | Works when |
|---|---|---|
| **Benefit** | the outcome the buyer gets | the outcome is concrete and quickly stated |
| **Pain** | the problem they already have | the problem is felt daily and is the reader's own |
| **Time** | what they stop doing | the task being replaced is tedious and familiar |
| **Loss** | what they missed, or will | the cost of missing is real and non-financial |
| **Proof** | evidence, numbers, names | the numbers are verifiable and yours |
| **Objection** | the reason they hesitate | there is one obvious, nameable blocker |
| **Category re-frame** | a different way to see the choice | the default framing is genuinely wrong |
| **Niche expert** | doing one thing only | specialisation is real and checkable |
| **Target filter** | who it is NOT for | narrowing raises quality more than volume |
| **Scorekeeper** | specific figures as trust signals | every figure has a source |
| **Speed** | how fast the result arrives | the speed is a workflow fact, not a promise about outcomes |
| **Urgency / scarcity** | a real deadline or limit | it is true. Otherwise never. |

The last twelve rows are a merge of two public taxonomies with duplicates collapsed; **Time**,
**Loss** and **Target filter** are the ones most often missing from a set, and **Target filter** —
an ad whose job is to *exclude* people — is the one least likely to be reached for unprompted.

## Hook quality

- One idea per hook. If it needs a comma-spliced second clause, it is two hooks.
- Lead with the buyer's outcome or problem. The brand name goes in the lockup, not the first words.
- Specifics beat adjectives: "scores every disclosure 0–100" over "powerful analysis".

## Message-match

Every unit must echo a claim the **landing page actually makes**. Record which one. A unit with no
matching destination claim is either cut, or the page gap is reported. A mismatch is a wasted click
and a claim-integrity risk at once.

## Claim and policy pre-check

- **Claims:** superlatives, any number, any performance or earnings statement needs a source. No
  source means it does not ship as fact.
- **Policy:** regulated verticals (finance, health, credit), competitor trademarks, before/after
  framing, and sensitive-attribute targeting language all carry platform and legal risk. Flag them;
  never quietly delete a user-supplied claim — say why it is risky.
- **In a regulated market, the pain angle drifts hardest.** Keep the pain about the work, not about
  losing money.
